Pariqo
Executive Summary
What is Pariqo?
Pariqo is a digital platform designed to connect creative & marketing agencies. It enables them to showcase services in a curated marketplace, find and partner with complementary agencies, and collaborate directly via project spaces (tasks, messaging, files). Over time, it will also facilitate tendered work (“pitches”) and integrate subscription/payment features.

Core Components: Pillar
What It Enables
Market
Agencies advertise service offerings; brands or other agencies browse/search service packages or custom listings.

Match
Agencies discover, request, and accept partnerships—either to share workload, refer business, or collaborate. Projects & Collaboration Once matched, agencies manage joint work via shared projects: (Kanban) tasks, messaging, file exchanges.

Pitch / Tenders (Stage Two) Larger organizations post tenders; agencies submit bids, often in teams.

Payments & Subscriptions Monetizing through subscriptions and/or referral fees; future-stage revenue flows.

Target Users & Market: Segment
Pariqo Addresses Small to Mid-Sized Agencies
Need visibility; can’t always sustain large workstreams alone; want partnerships. Specialist Agencies / Niche Providers Want exposure to projects they don’t fully own; prefer collaboration. Brands / Enterprises Need a marketplace to discover verified agencies; easier to manage RFPs (“pitches”). Referral-Partners Agencies wanting to refer or subcontract; benefit from structured tracking and revenue share.

Modular roadmap: launch core Market + Match + Projects first, then add tenders and payments.
